# TraceFold Preservation Invariants

Status: Phase 0 freeze

Invariant registry version: `1.0.0`

This registry defines the minimum hard obligations that supported analyzers must discover and that the independent verifier must check. It operationalizes `productSpec.md` §§5, 8, 9, 20 and `buildPlan.md` §§1, 6, 7, and 9.

The required class lists come from the Phase 0 task and the cited source sections. **[PROPOSED PROJECT DECISION]** The canonical names, operational detection/exactness rules, ID forms, source-location minima, and recovery rules below are proposed project decisions that make those classes testable.

## Normative conventions

- Every discovered invariant has a stable obligation ID, class, typed value, detector provenance, applicability status, and one or more source spans.
- **[PROPOSED PROJECT DECISION]** IDs are content-bound: `obl:<canonical-name>:<sha256-of-canonical-class-value-and-source-refs>` and `rel:<canonical-name>:<sha256-of-canonical-endpoints-and-source-refs>`.
- Original byte ranges are authoritative. Ranges are zero-based, half-open UTF-8 byte intervals. Character and line/column coordinates follow `source-map-schema.md`.
- “Exact” means equality after only the normalization explicitly allowed in the row. It never means semantic similarity.
- Required relations are verified as typed endpoint associations, not by checking that endpoint strings occur independently.
- Detection may use overlapping detectors. Detector agreement improves discovery confidence but does not replace independent verification.
- When a required detector is unavailable or applicability is uncertain, discovery is `unknown`; the certificate cannot claim complete preservation.
- Recovery is monotonic: restore the smallest exact source span that closes the invariant, then regenerate mappings and reverify. If this is impossible within policy, expand budget or use full fallback.

## Exactness levels

- **Byte-exact:** identical UTF-8 byte sequence from the original source.
- **Lexeme-exact:** exact original lexical form after line-ending normalization only; case, sign, punctuation, and qualifiers remain unchanged.
- **Typed-value exact:** parser-normalized value equality plus preservation of the original lexeme in provenance.
- **Authority-exact:** role, message, and instruction scope remain unchanged.
- **Relation-exact:** typed endpoints and direction/scope remain connected.
- **Structural-exact:** parse structure or path identity is equivalent under a declared deterministic structural encoding.

## Cross-cutting verifier rules

1. Presence-only checking is invalid for relation-bearing obligations.
2. The verifier independently derives obligations from the original and candidate artifact; compressor counts are not evidence.
3. The verifier compares canonical typed observations and source-bound lexemes, not embeddings or free-form similarity.
4. Parser disagreement, unresolved authority, stale maps, missing required analyzers, or ambiguous protected relations yield `indeterminate`, never `passed`.
5. An emitted synthesized span may support lineage but cannot satisfy byte-exact obligations unless the exact protected lexeme is also present and mapped.
6. `full_fallback` verifies artifact/hash identity and role framing; it does not claim that obligation discovery was complete.

## Completeness rule

**[PROPOSED PROJECT DECISION]** The registry uses three discovery states per applicable content type:

- `known`: every required detector ran successfully and the format is within its declared support envelope;
- `partial`: at least one detector produced usable observations but some required coverage is missing;
- `unknown`: applicability, parsing, or required discovery failed.

Certificate completeness is:

- `complete` only when every applicable content type is `known`, every required obligation/relation is independently verified, and no hard invariant failed;
- `partial` when known discovered items are verified but discovery is partial;
- `unknown` when applicability or discovery is unknown.

`certificate_coverage = verified_discovered / verifier_discovered` is never a substitute for discovery completeness.
